1. What Shuleka is, and who can do what

Shuleka is the system your school uses to keep class lists, staff records, assessments, marks, and CBE report cards in one place. You do not install anything. You open it in a web browser (Chrome, Edge, or similar) on a computer, laptop, or phone.

Your school is set up for you. There is no self-signup. A school admin (or Shuleka Tech) creates your account, then you sign in.

The four kinds of account

Role What you see Typical jobs
Teacher Dashboard, Exams, and My class if you are a class teacher. You do not see Students, Staff, Subjects, or Class Setup. Enter marks for the classes you teach. Class teachers also view their learners.
School admin The full school menu, locked to your school. School name appears at the top of every page. Students, staff, subjects, class setup, exams, reports, and password resets for staff in that school.
Super admin Extra items: Schools and Accounts. You only see schools the system admin ticked for you. Super admins cannot teach. Work across assigned schools. Promote a teacher to school admin. Cannot make another super admin.
System admin Every school and every user. Banner says “System Administrator” / Full Access. Add schools, delete a school or a subject, make or remove super admins, and tick which schools each super may open.
Tip. If a button is missing, you are probably on a teacher account. Ask your school admin to do that job, or to make you an admin if that is your role.

2. Open the website and sign in

  1. Open your browser and go to the Shuleka address your school gave you (the public site looks like the page below).
  2. Click Sign in at the top right, or the Sign in button in the middle of the page.
  1. On Welcome to Shuleka, type your Username and Password.
  2. Optional: tick Remember me on a computer only you use.
  3. Click the red Sign in button.

Your first username and password

  • When a school admin adds you under School Staff, Shuleka creates a username from your name and the last four digits of your phone.
  • Your first password is usually those same last four digits of your phone number (for example, if your phone is 0712 345 678, try 5678).
  • Change that password the first time you sign in (see chapter 12).
Forgot password? Click Forgot password? on the sign-in page. Shuleka will tell you to ask your school admin to reset it, or to call Shuleka Tech. There is no email reset link.
Wrong password. The page says “Invalid username or password”. Check caps lock, try the last four phone digits, then ask admin to use Reset password on Accounts or on your staff profile.

3. Find your way around

After you sign in you always see the same frame: coral sidebar on the left, search at the top (admins), your school name in a dark bar, then the page in the middle.

DashboardSee counts of students and staff, and a welcome screen.

My classOnly if you are a class teacher (and not a super admin).
StudentsClass lists, add or import learners, open a profile.

SubjectsLearning areas offered in the school.

On a phone the same items sit in a compact bar. Look for the same names.

Search for a student (admins)

  1. Click the long search box at the top: Search by name, admission number, or assessment number.
  2. Type part of the name, the admission number, or the KNEC assessment number, then press Enter or click the arrow.
  3. A Student Search window lists matches. Open the student you want.
  4. Press Esc or Close to dismiss the window.
Shortcut. On a computer you can jump to search with Ctrl+K (Windows) or ⌘K (Mac).

Your account menu

Click your name or photo at the top right. You can Update Photo, Change password, or Sign Out. Use Logout at the bottom of the sidebar for the same sign-out.

4. Dashboard

School admins land on Dashboard — “Overview & system activity”. Teachers land on Teacher Portal instead (chapter 10).

  • The welcome line uses your full name.
  • Cards show how many Students, Teachers, Staff, and (for some accounts) branches.
  • Your school motto appears under the school name.
  • A coloured badge next to the school name may show subscription status (Active, Due soon, or Overdue). You can still work; the office uses it for billing.

5. Students

Click Students in the sidebar. The first time, the list is empty until you load a class.

Load a class list

  1. Under Load class list, open Grade / Form and pick the class (for example GRADE 7).
  2. If that class has streams, a Stream box appears. Pick A, B, and so on.
  3. School admins do not pick a school — you already belong to one. Super/system admins also choose School first.
  4. Click Load students.

The table shows admission number, name, gender, assessment number, date of birth, and parent phone. Use Search this class to filter the table without leaving the page.

Add one student

  1. Click Add student.
  2. Choose Grade / Form (and stream if asked).
  3. Admission No. is usually filled with the next free number. Change it only if your school uses a different sequence.
  4. Fill Full Name and Gender. Add date of birth, parent name, and parent phone when you have them.
  5. Optional fields (NEMIS, KNEC, KCPE, boarding, pathway) can wait.
  6. Click Add student at the bottom of the window, or Cancel to close without saving.

Import many students from Excel

  1. Click Import list.
  2. Read the two panels. Column A header must be Adm No, column B Fullname.
  3. Click Download sample spreadsheet if you want a ready file.
  4. Start admission numbers at the Next admission number shown (duplicates are skipped).
  5. Choose Grade / Form, click Choose File, pick an .xls or .xlsx file, then Upload students.

Export the class you loaded

After a class is on screen, click Export list to download that class as Excel.

Move several students to another class

  1. Tick the boxes next to the learners (or Select all).
  2. Click Move selected (wording: the bar says how many are selected, then open Move).
  3. Choose the destination class. Admission numbers do not change. This cannot be undone from that screen.

Open a student profile

In the class table, open the row menu (three dots) and choose Profile, or use top search. You can also click through from search results.

  • Update passport — photo.
  • Edit then Save Changes — name, contacts, KCPE, and similar.
  • Move — send this one learner to another grade or stream.
  • Delete — permanent. Confirm only if you are sure.
  • Academics tab — exam results and options to print a report card (with or without class position; CBE often prints without positions).

6. School staff

Click School Staff. The Teachers tab lists teaching staff. ADMIN on a name means that person can open the admin menus. Support staff is a separate tab.

  1. Click Add teacher.
  2. Choose employer prefix if you use a teacher code (TSC/BOM), then optional code such as T12.
  3. Set Gender, title (Mr., Mrs., …), and Fullname.
  4. Phone number is required (0712345678 or +254712345678). This drives the first password.
  5. Optional: National ID, TSC number, email.
  6. Click Save teacher.

Use Export list to download staff as Excel. Open Profile on a row to see details, reset a password (if you are allowed), or upload a photo.

Do not click Make super on a real class teacher unless you mean it. Promoting to super admin removes their teaching load and class-teacher assignments.

7. Subjects & learning areas

Click Subjects. You see codes, names, categories, which classes take the subject, and whether it is compulsory.

  1. Click Add subject.
  2. Enter name (for example English), code (for example 121), and category.
  3. Tick Examinable / Compulsory as your school requires.
  4. Tick the grades that offer this learning area, or All classes.
  5. Save. Use the pencil to edit later.

View by grade shows which learning areas sit on each grade. Deleting a subject is allowed only for a system admin.

8. Class setup

Click Class Setup. This is where the school structure lives. Do this before a busy exam week: grades first, then subjects on students, then teachers.

Overview — add a grade or form

  1. Click Add grade / form.
  2. Set class year (for example 2026), grade/form name (Grade 7 or Form 2), and optional streams separated by commas (A, B).
  3. Save. Use Edit to rename; Delete only if the class is empty or you accept the warning.

Student subjects

Open the Student subjects tab. Choose a class (and subject). Tick the learners who take that subject. Compulsory subjects are usually already ticked. Save when the ticks are right. Senior school (for example Grade 10) may need this more often because of pathways.

Assign teachers

Open Assign teachers. Choose the grade. For each subject and stream, pick a teacher from the list. Super admins do not appear as teachers. Click save when the page confirms “Teachers for this class have been updated.”

Manage class teachers

Open Manage class teachers. Choose the grade, then pick one teacher per stream. That person gets My class in their sidebar. A stream marked Unassigned on Overview still needs this step.

9. Exams: create, enter marks, print reports

Click Exams. Admins see five tabs. Teachers see a shorter page: pick an open exam and enter marks.

Create an exam (admin)

  1. On Exam Setup, click Create New Exam/Assessment (or the + button on the right).
  2. Choose school (if you can see more than one), academic year, term (I, II, or III), and exam name (for example Mid Term Assessment).
  3. Optional: set Teachers' marks entry deadline so a banner reminds teachers.
  4. Save. The exam appears with status Open.

On the exam row:

  • Enter Marks — open the mark sheet.
  • ⋮ menu — Edit, Set teachers' marks entry deadline, Lock Exam, or Delete Exam.
Lock Exam stops all further mark entry. Unlock later with Unlock Exam if you must correct a paper. Delete Exam wipes every mark for that exam. There is no undo.

Tick Show archived if you need old exams that were put away.

Enter marks

  1. Click Enter Marks on the exam, or open Exams as a teacher and pick the exam.
  2. Stay on By subject (class markbook) or switch to By learner (one student, all papers).
  3. Choose Class, then Stream if asked, then Subject.
  4. Type scores in MARKS / 100 (or whatever “out of” is set). Marks save as you work when the page allows saving.
  5. Use Back to Exams when finished.
If the exam is locked, or the deadline has passed, teachers see a closed message. Admins may still view marks but teachers cannot save.

Subjects in the list are those assigned in Class Setup → Assign teachers. If English is missing, the teacher was not assigned that lesson.

Results & reports

  1. Open the Results & Reports tab.
  2. Select Grade / Class, Stream (or All), and Exam.
  3. Click Fetch Results.
  4. From there you can open class ranking and generate report cards. When asked about positions, CBE schools often choose No — Print without positions (CBE Recommended).
  5. Optional: tick Include opening date and set the date so reports print “School opens on …”.

Grading & rules

Open Grading & Rules. Assign the right scheme to each grade: 4-band rubric (primary), 8-point KJSEA scale (junior secondary), senior school scale, or 8-4-4. Without a scheme, reports may show scores without performance levels (EE1, ME1, and so on).

Marksheets & classlists / Broadsheets

Use Marksheets & Classlists to print blank or filled mark sheets. Use Broadsheets & Analytics for whole-class analysis and Excel/PDF downloads after marks are in.

10. If you are a teacher (not an admin)

Sign in the same way. Shuleka sends you to Teacher Portal, not the admin dashboard.

  • The sidebar is short: Dashboard, Exams, and My class if you are a class teacher.
  • The welcome card lists how many classes you teach. Click Enter Marks.
  • Classes & Subjects shows your teaching load (class, stream, subject, headcount).
  • You cannot add students or create exams. Ask your school admin.

On Exams you only see exams that are still Open. If the list is empty, either no exam exists yet, or the last one was locked.

11. My class (class teachers)

You see My class only after an admin assigns you under Class Setup → Manage class teachers. Super admins never get this menu.

  1. Click My class.
  2. If you have more than one stream, pick the chip for that class.
  3. Learners — register: names, contacts, missing parent phones.
  4. Open a learner for a simple profile and Back to my class.
  5. Exam results — pick an exam to see marks for your class.
  6. Other tabs cover class performance for that exam.
If you see “You are not assigned as a class teacher”, ask admin to set you on Class Setup → Manage class teachers.

12. Your password and photo

  1. Open the account menu (your name, top right) → Change password.
  2. Type current password, new password (at least 6 characters), and confirm.
  3. Click Update password.

Update Photo in the same menu uploads a passport picture. Admins can also reset another person’s password from Accounts or the teacher profile, within their school. Nobody can reset their own password that way (use this page instead). School admins cannot reset a super admin or system admin.

14. Schools and Accounts (super admin / system admin)

These two sidebar items appear only if you are a super admin or system admin.

Schools

  1. Click Schools.
  2. Choose a school in Select branch / school to view details.
  3. Use Add to register a new school, Edit to change name, motto, logo, manager, and similar, and Delete only if you are a system admin and you understand that school data will go.

Accounts

Click Accounts. Filter by All, Admin, Super admin, Teacher (and System admin if you are one). Search by name, username, phone, or school.

ButtonWho can use itWhat it does
Make admin / Remove adminSuper admin, system adminLets a teacher use Students, Staff, Exams setup, and the rest of the school menu — still in their school.
Make super / Remove superSystem admin onlyCreates a super admin with no schools ticked until you save schools. Teaching assignments are cleared.
Save schoolsSystem adminTicks which schools that super may open. If none are ticked, that super sees nothing.

A super admin with no schools ticked is not a bug. The system admin must open Accounts, tick schools, and click Save schools.

Page looks tiny on a phoneTurn the phone sideways, or use a computer for Excel import and broadsheets.

A simple weekly rhythm

  1. Keep Class Setup and student lists accurate (moves, new admissions).
  2. Create the term’s exam and tell teachers the deadline.
  3. Teachers enter marks. Admin checks missing marks, then Lock Exam.
  4. Fetch Results, print report cards, share with class teachers.
  5. Sign out.
